READING KNIGHT: PINK CASTLE DEFENSE

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Keep index.html, game.js, styles.css, and the assets folder together.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
4. Choose 1st Grade or 3rd Grade Reading.
5. Click the goblin/answer board with the correct answer.

SCORING
- Correct on the first try: 100 points
- Correct on the second try: 50 points
- Correct on the third try: 25 points
- 10 rounds per game

FEATURES
- 160 reading questions total (80 per level)
- Questions are randomized and stored in browser localStorage so they do not repeat until the level's pool cycles
- Generated painted art assets for the pink castle scene, knight, three goblins, parchment question banner, answer boards, and tip sign
- Canvas rendering and simple attack/hit animations
- Built-in sound effects via Web Audio; no audio files required
- Sound toggle and restart controls

No internet connection is required after download.
